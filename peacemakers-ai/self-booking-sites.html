The shift

What changed on Airbnb

Airbnb moved from a split fee model — roughly 3% from hosts and 12–14% from guests — to a single 15.5% host-only service fee, shifted entirely onto hosts starting October 2025. For many PMS-connected accounts, rollout continued through 2026.

The offer

Direct Rebook Kit

A simple setup for hosts who want past guests to rebook directly — branded site, in-property QR card, and guest email capture. No revenue share. No lock-in.

Part 1

Your own booking site

A branded, mobile-first page with real availability — so guests who already trust you can book again without going back through the OTA. You set the rate. You keep the relationship.

Part 2

QR / tap-to-book card for the rental

A print-ready card guests see at checkout — scan or tap to save your direct booking link. That's how you turn a great Airbnb stay into a fee-free return visit.

Part 3

Simple guest email capture

A lightweight way to collect emails from guests who opt in — so you can reach them again without paying 15.5% to do it. No obligation to run campaigns. The list is yours.

What this is not

Not a "replace Airbnb" play. Airbnb still brings first-time discovery. This is for guests who already stayed with you and would happily come back.

Why it works

The math is gentle — but real

Every direct booking skips the 15.5% fee entirely. That gives you room to keep the full nightly rate as extra profit, or price slightly below your Airbnb listing and still net more per stay.

On that $115 example, $17.80 stays in your pocket instead of Airbnb's. Multiply that across repeat guests over a season and it adds up fast — without touching your discovery traffic.

Two ways hosts use the margin

Keep your Airbnb rate on direct bookings → more profit per night.

Or price a little lower on direct → guest saves, you still earn more than the OTA payout. Win-win for repeat visitors.
