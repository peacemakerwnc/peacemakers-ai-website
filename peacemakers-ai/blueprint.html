Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to already be using AI?

No. We start with your process and current tools. Where AI is appropriate, we identify practical opportunities with clear human controls.

Do you implement solutions too?

Yes—if you choose to move forward with one or more recommendations, implementation is scoped and priced separately after the Blueprint.

What if I only want the Blueprint?

That is completely fine. You can use the written recommendations internally, or later decide whether any implementation support is needed.

Will this replace my team?

No. The goal is to reduce repetitive work, improve consistency, and keep people in control of important decisions.

How do you handle data security and sensitive information?

We use a client-controlled approach. You own systems, licenses, accounts, approvals, and final decisions. We avoid unnecessary sensitive data, request least-access, and require written approval before material changes.
