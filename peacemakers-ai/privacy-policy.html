Privacy Policy

What this website collects, which tools run on it, and how to make a privacy request.

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy describes what happens to information when you use peacemakersai.com, the website of Peacemakers AI Solutions, LLC (“Peacemakers AI,” “we,” and “us”), a North Carolina limited liability company. The public site describes the business as based in Asheville, North Carolina. This page does not list a street address or a phone number. Use of the site is also covered by our Terms of Service.

Questions: james@peacemakersai.com.

Information you give us

The site has no user accounts. We receive what you type into a form or send by email.

Contact form

The contact page asks for your name, email, an optional business name, and a message.

Home, Blueprint, and checklist forms

The home page, the Blueprint page, and the local-service checklist form ask for your name, email, industry, and a short description of what you need help with. Phone is optional. The form also sends the page you were on, the time of the submission, and, when they were in the link you opened, the campaign parameters utm_source, utm_medium, and utm_campaign.

Early access

The early-access form asks for those contact details and also business name, website, timeline, what you are interested in, and whether you want a feedback call. A required checkbox says we may contact you about early access, Blueprint availability, and related AI implementation services.

AI Opportunity Scorecard

The scorecard asks for your name, email, optional phone, company name, and website, plus your answers: industry, team size, tools you use, which categories of sensitive information your business handles, any security or compliance requirements you want us to know about, timeline, readiness, and the score the page calculates. The sensitive-information question is a checklist of categories. It does not ask you to upload customer, employee, medical, student, or payment records.

Blueprint security notes

The Blueprint form asks the same kind of contact details as the home page form. It also asks which categories of sensitive information your business handles and whether you have security or compliance requirements. Those are categories and a note you type, not copies of the records.

Article and starter-kit forms

Many article and resource pages ask for the email address you type. Several of those forms also send fixed values the page fills in, such as a placeholder name, an industry label, and a note that you requested a starter kit. If you email james@peacemakersai.com, we receive whatever you put in that message.

Scheduling

Booking links open Calendly at calendly.com/james-peacemakersai. The time and contact details you enter there are collected by Calendly.

Information collected while you browse

The main marketing pages, including the home page, services, locations, industries, contact, and this policy, load the following tools when the page loads:

  • Google Tag Manager, container GTM-M5L8TPDF.
  • Google Analytics 4, measurement ID G-GTQ5YNER7P, using the standard gtag.js configuration. The snippet does not add extra privacy settings.
  • Microsoft Clarity, project wgrv405eqy, loaded by that Tag Manager container. Clarity is a session-recording and heatmap product. A recording can include how a visit moved through a page, such as clicks and scrolling, along with usage statistics.

On October 28, 2026, the published Tag Manager container had one tag: Microsoft Clarity for project wgrv405eqy. That tag runs when Tag Manager loads and is set to receive the Google Analytics client ID and session ID. The page does not also load Clarity on its own. Tags inside Tag Manager can be changed in Google’s account without changing this website. A new tag would not be described here until this policy is updated.

The current blog article template does not include Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, or Clarity. Those article pages still load the shared site script below.

The published pages do not include a Meta (Facebook) pixel, Hotjar tag, or HubSpot tracker. A Facebook pixel example in the site script is commented out and does not run. LinkedIn and Facebook addresses appear as links or in page metadata.

Other scripts

  • The shared site script (script.js) runs on most pages. It does not set its own cookies, and it does not store your light or dark theme after you leave the page. When Google Analytics is on the page and you use the “Add Peacemakers AI as a Preferred Source” link, the script sends a Google Analytics event named preferred_source_click.
  • That script loads Google’s Preferred Source button from news.google.com/swg/js/v1/publisher.js.
  • Calendly’s widget script is included in the HTML of some pages, so it loads with the page. Other booking buttons load Calendly when you click them, or they open calendly.com in a new tab.
  • Many marketing pages load the Inter and Source Serif 4 typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com. Your browser requests those files from Google.
  • The site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel receives ordinary server logs, such as IP address, browser, and the page requested, to run and protect the site. The site code does not include Vercel Web Analytics or Speed Insights.

Cookies

Our site script does not set cookies. Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity, Calendly when it loads, and Google’s Preferred Source script may set cookies or similar identifiers. Common examples are Analytics cookies such as _ga, and Clarity cookies such as _clck and _clsk. Vendors can change those names.

The site has no cookie banner. On pages that include them, Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, and Clarity run when the page loads. Microsoft requires a consent signal before Clarity uses its cookies for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland. This site does not send that signal, so Clarity limits cookies and some recordings for those visitors. For other visitors, Clarity can record the session when the page loads.

Why we use this information

  • To reply to a question and to send a resource you asked for.
  • To follow up about early access or a Blueprint conversation when you asked us to contact you.
  • To see which pages are used and how they behave, including Analytics measurements and Clarity recordings and heatmaps, so we can improve the site.
  • To operate, secure, and host the site.

Who receives information

We do not sell personal information. The site has no integration that sells information to data brokers.

Every form on this site submits to Formspree (formspree.io, form maqaaddz). Formspree stores the submission and can email it to the address set in that Formspree account. The website code does not call MailerLite, ConvertKit, Make.com, or Google Sheets. Internal setup notes describe a possible later path from Formspree to Make to MailerLite to Google Sheets for the local-service checklist. Those notes are instructions for a connection that is not in the website, and the checklist page still says email-platform delivery is not connected. Those tools are not part of what the site does today.

These companies receive information because of how the site is built:

  • Formspree, for form submissions
  • Google, for Analytics, Tag Manager, Fonts, and the Preferred Source script
  • Microsoft, for Clarity
  • Calendly, when you open the scheduler
  • Vercel, for hosting

Each of those companies has its own privacy policy. LinkedIn and the Amazon listing on the books page are other websites you choose to open. We do not embed Amazon or LinkedIn tracking on our pages. This website does not take payment. A book linked from the books page is sold on Amazon under Amazon’s terms.

We may also share information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

The website code does not contain a deletion timer. The periods below are either the tool’s own rule or a choice we are stating here.

Form submissions

Formspree’s free plan keeps a submission archive for 30 days. Paid Formspree plans keep the archive until someone deletes it. This website does not record which plan form maqaaddz uses, so the archive length follows that plan. It is not a date in the page. Formspree also emails the submission to the inbox configured on the form. We keep that email so we can reply. Our choice, which is not a switch in the website or in Formspree, is to delete an inquiry email within 24 months when it never became a client engagement. Messages that become part of a client engagement stay with that engagement.

Google Analytics

Google’s default for event and user data on a standard Analytics property is 2 months. The account owner can change that to 14 months under Admin, Data settings, Data retention. That choice is in the Analytics account. It is not set in this website, and this policy assumes the 2-month default unless that account setting has been changed. The setting affects explorations and funnel reports. Google’s standard aggregate reports can remain available longer. Age, gender, and interest data stay on the 2-month period even if the other setting is longer.

Microsoft Clarity

These periods are Clarity’s. They are not a setting in this website. Session playback is kept for 30 days. Heatmap and click data are kept for 9 months. Recordings that are labeled or marked as favorites, and a small sample of older recordings, are kept for up to 9 months.

Other tools

Google Tag Manager, Google Fonts, Calendly, and Vercel keep data under their own policies. This website does not set those periods. Vercel hosting logs, such as IP address and the page requested, follow Vercel’s log retention.

Your choices and requests

Email james@peacemakersai.com to ask for a copy of information you submitted, a correction, or deletion. We will handle a request we can match to a submission. The site has no automated privacy portal.

You can also limit analytics in your browser:

  • Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Microsoft Clarity opt-out: clarity.microsoft.com/opt-out
  • Browser settings can block or delete cookies. Blocking cookies can affect the scheduler and forms.

The site does not look for a Do Not Track signal. Opting out of Analytics does not by itself stop Clarity.

Where you live may give you other privacy rights. This page describes the tools the site uses. It is not a full restatement of every privacy statute.

Children

The consulting pages are written for business owners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The books page describes a coloring book for children and links to Amazon. That page does not ask a child to send us information. The scorecard lets a business say whether it handles student or minor information. That is a category the business selects. It is not a form for a child.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and forms are submitted to Formspree over HTTPS. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date.
