The shift

What changed on Airbnb

Airbnb moved from a split fee (~3% host / 12–14% guest) to a single 15.5% host-only service fee — shifted entirely onto hosts starting October 2025. For many PMS-connected accounts, rollout continued through 2026.

Why it works

Skip the fee on repeat stays

Every direct booking avoids the 15.5% fee. Keep your full rate as extra profit — or price slightly below your Airbnb listing and still net more per night.

On a $115 night, that's $17.80 back in your pocket. Multiply that across return guests over a season.

Two common approaches

Match your Airbnb rate on direct → higher margin.

Price a little lower on direct → guest saves, you still beat the OTA payout.
