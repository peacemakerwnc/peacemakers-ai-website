The shift

What changed on Airbnb

Airbnb moved from a split fee (~3% host / 12–14% guest) to a single 15.5% host-only service fee. For many PMS-connected accounts, rollout began October 2025 and continued through 2026.

$115 booking example

Guest pays$115.00
Airbnb host fee (15.5%)− $17.80
You keep (before your costs)$97.20

Cleaning fees can also be subject to the host-side deduction — not just the nightly rate.

That fee is unavoidable on Airbnb bookings. It's completely avoidable on direct ones.

Why it works

Skip the fee on repeat stays

Every direct booking avoids the 15.5% fee. Keep your full rate as extra profit — or price slightly below your Airbnb listing and still net more per night.

On a $115 night, that's $17.80 back in your pocket. Multiply that across return guests over a season.

Two common approaches

Match your Airbnb rate on direct → higher margin.

Price a little lower on direct → guest saves, you still beat the OTA payout.
