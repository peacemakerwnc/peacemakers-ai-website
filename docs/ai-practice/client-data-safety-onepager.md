# How we keep your data safe

**Peacemakers AI · a page you can hand to a client**

You stay in charge of your business. We help you use AI on the paperwork you already have: estimates, selections, inspection notes, forms, and the reports your people retype today. We do that in a way you can explain to a customer, a lender, or your insurance agent.

This page says how we handle your information. It is not a contract, and it is not legal advice. Your agreement, and your own lawyer, control if something here ever disagrees with them.

## Your files stay yours

You own your records, your customer lists, your prices, and your accounts. When a tool needs a license, you buy it in your company's name when that is practical. We ask only for the access the job actually needs, and we stop using it when the job is done.

## We take personal details out before a model sees the file

A proposal does not need a Social Security number, a bank account, a card number, or a wire instruction. Before notes go to a model, we remove the contact details and account numbers the model does not need to do the work. The name and the service address can stay on your computer and go back on the finished PDF after a person has checked the draft.

We do not paste your files into a personal chatbot login.

## A person approves anything a customer would see

The model can pull quantities and line items out of a messy note. Your rate card sets the price. Someone at your company, or at ours with your say-so, reads the draft before it is sent. AI does not email a customer, change a bill, or move money.

## We do not use your work to train a public model

For production work we use business or API accounts whose terms say the vendor does not train on your content unless you opt in. Where the vendor offers a zero-retention option and the work calls for it, we turn that on. Consumer chat accounts are a different product. They are not where client files go.

## We do not keep a copy we do not need

We keep what the job requires, for as long as the job requires, and then we delete it. Logs record that a step happened. They do not need to hold the whole document. If you want the working files to live only in your systems, that is the default we aim for.

## If something goes wrong

You will hear from us. If personal information of a North Carolina resident is involved, North Carolina's breach-notice rules may require notice to the people affected and to the Attorney General. We will help you gather what happened. We will not hide it, and we will not send that notice for you unless you have asked us to and your lawyer is in the loop.

## What we need from you

Tell us what is sensitive before we start. Customer contacts, employee records, medical notes, card numbers, insurance files, and closing files are not "just paperwork." Point us at a person who can approve a draft. Tell us which system is the real record, whether that is a spreadsheet, your inbox, or NetSuite.

## What this page is not

It is not a promise that a tool will be perfect. It is not a promise of a dollar savings. It is not a substitute for your policies, your insurance, or your lawyer. If we build something for you, the statement of work will say what data it touches, who approves the output, and how long anything is kept.
