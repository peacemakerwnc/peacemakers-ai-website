A view does not ask for an estimate.

If you run a building, contracting, or home-service firm in North Carolina, the number on the post is not the number that books the work. Write down what a qualified inquiry is for your firm, then count those — and count the appointments they keep.

## Left out on purpose

- Viralish's earnings, follower count, view counts, and the "up to 20x" line. Those are influencer-reported. [PR #37](https://github.com/peacemakerwnc/peacemakers-ai-website/pull/37) already marked them unaudited, and the "45% didn't count" line contradicts the slide's own 2.96 million / 11.7 million.
- Any promise that views, inquiries, or appointments will double, and any refund promise.
- A comment-keyword close. That is a separate system, drafted in `docs/specs/comment-keyword-auto-reply.md`. This caption uses links.
- A Facebook-creator-payout essay. Meta does pay creators on qualified views and watch time, not on every view, and watch time under 5 seconds does not qualify ([About Facebook Content Monetization](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1049081556813520), as checked in PR #37). That rule is the analogy. It is not this post.
