## 1. Eagles Nest Outfitters (ENO)

### Snapshot

ENO makes hammocks and related outdoor gear at 601 Sweeten Creek Industrial Park, Asheville. The public site prints the phone (828) 252-7808 and office hours Monday–Friday, 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. EST.

The Citizen Times story of 5 July 2024, cited in the first memo, names Pete Pinholster as co-founder and sole owner. That article was paywalled on a re-fetch today, so this note does not add new quotes from it. A directory (SignalHire, updated date shown as 23 September 2026) lists other executive names, including a different CEO. That directory conflicts with the 2024 newspaper account. It was not used as an org chart, and no directory email was copied.

The person who would feel this particular gap is Anna Rawlins. Her public LinkedIn title is Chief Marketing Officer and VP of Sales at ENO. The open job on ENO's own careers page reports to "the Chief Marketing Officer & VP of Sales" and works beside an Art Director and a Content Marketing Manager. Her short LinkedIn about-text describes sales, marketing, and events. It does not describe a consulting practice. Her full profile did not load in an earlier fetch; the title and the short about-text are what this pass could read.

Size is still an estimate, and the estimates disagree. Adapt.io, cited in the first memo, puts revenue in a $10–50 million range and headcount in a 25–100 range. A LinkedIn snippet in that memo said about $27 million and 21 employees. SignalHire says 10–50 employees and $10–50 million. None of those are ENO financials. The honest read is a single-site Asheville manufacturer inside or near the target band, owner-led as of the 2024 article, with a staffed marketing and customer-service bench.

Best first contact for this subject: (828) 252-7808, and ask for Pete or for the marketing and sales lead. The careers inbox jobs@enonation.com is for applicants, not for a sales pitch. bobby@enonation.com remains the authorized-retailer address on the bulk page, not a path to the owner.

### Top 3 gaps

**1. New retailers cannot start an account on the page that offers one.**

The B2B login page says to sign in for ordering and to apply on the form below: https://eaglesnestoutfittersinc.com/pages/b2b-login. The application body did not render in the fetch. The wholesale signup URL says, in full, "Wholesale sign up form is currently unavailable. Please check after some time." https://eaglesnestoutfittersinc.com/apps/wpdapp

That is a stopped door on the channel ENO is trying to grow. Dollar impact is unknown. No visitor count, close rate, or average wholesale order was published. Assumption, labeled as such: if the open role's main duty is new wholesale accounts, a signup form that tells people to come back later is sitting on that same work. This is not a revenue calculation.

**2. Account setup, the first order, and bulk quoting are written as a person's job.**

ENO's careers page, checked 23 October 2026, has one full-time Asheville role, open until filled: Marketing and Business Development Manager. Apply at jobs@enonation.com. The split on the page is marketing execution 20%, channel ownership 20%, and new business development 60%. Duties include guiding prospects through account setup and initial orders, and "Support ENO's bulk and co-brand program, including prospecting, inbound inquiries, quoting, project coordination, and repeat business opportunities." The same post says to maintain the pipeline in "ENO's sales systems." https://eaglesnestoutfittersinc.com/pages/job-opportunities

Hours impact, as an assumption: 60% of a full-time week is about 24 hours. If even a quarter of that slice is repetitive account setup and quoting, that is about 6 hours a week of a skilled hire. ENO did not publish a wage. The page says compensation is based on experience. A job-board salary range seen elsewhere was not on ENO's page and is not used.

**3. The bulk-gift path still waits one to three business days, and the office closes at 4.**

The bulk page still says applications are processed by email, "Please allow 1-3 business days," minimum ten hammocks, card payment only, no free shipping, U.S. addresses only, and "ENO does not currently have capabilities to embroider or customize at this time." https://eaglesnestoutfittersinc.com/pages/bulk-order-program

This is real, and it is smaller than gaps 1 and 2. The page itself keeps these orders out of resale. A nonprofit gift of ten hammocks is not the same problem as a resort or hardware account that cannot even apply.

This pass did not find a current public pattern of consumer complaints about shipping or order status worth weighting. Older forum posts were left out on purpose.

### Verdict on the first demo

Replace it. The first idea was a university club getting an instant yes or no against the bulk rules. Those rules are still true. The more valuable scene is a retailer who hits the unavailable wholesale form and needs account setup and a first-order quote. A bulk-rules demo would be accurate and would aim at the smaller door.

### Best demo, and what could follow

About 90 seconds. Open on the real wholesale signup page that says the form is unavailable. Cut to a sample retailer (a resort shop, obviously fictional) and a drafted account-setup note: what ENO already publishes (hours, Asheville address, no embroidery on the bulk path), what a person still has to approve (terms, first order, whether they belong in wholesale or in the bulk program), and a line that the reply is not sent until someone at ENO sends it. Real: the broken form, the careers-page duties, the bulk rules. Sample: the retailer and the draft. No price list.

A larger engagement, if they wanted one after seeing that, would be the wholesale application path itself: a working signup that lands in their existing sales system, plus a draft quote and account checklist for the human who already owns that job. It would sit beside Shopify, Klaviyo, and whatever sales system they already name. It would not replace those.

### Risks, overlap, red flags

They already run a real stack. The job post asks for working knowledge of Shopify, Klaviyo, GA4, Meta, Google Ads, TikTok, MNTN, QuickBooks Online, and spreadsheets. CRM experience is listed as nice to have, so this note does not claim they lack a CRM. They name customer service, finance, and leadership as people the new hire works with. Paid media already involves agency partners.

Anna Rawlins is an in-house marketing and sales lead, not a second firm selling AI implementation. No AI job title was found. The overlap risk is different: they may fix the form by hiring this person and calling an agency, and a cold demo will feel late if that hire is already the plan.

No acquisition or closure was found. Office hours stop at 4 p.m., which limits when a live walkthrough can happen. Do not email bobby@ or jobs@ as if either were Pete.

Studio hours on the first memo's contact read are Monday–Friday 9–5 at 93B Thompson Street, Asheville. Millwork hours are Monday–Thursday 6:30–5 at 108 Monticello Road, Suite 1400, Weaverville.

### Top 3 gaps

**1. They are hiring a designer whose job includes pricing proposals and the whole path to install.**

A Kitchen and Bath Cabinet Designer posting dated 6 August 2026 lists Forest Millwork in Asheville. Duties include meeting homeowners, builders, architects, and designers; layouts and renderings; "detailed cabinet designs, renderings, specifications, and pricing proposals"; vendor and production coordination; site visits and field measurements; and managing the job through ordering, delivery, and installation. Preferred tools include 2020 Design and Bluebeam. No salary is listed. The post says competitive salary plus commission. https://interior-design.thecreativeloft.com/interior-design/jobs/kitchen-cabinet-designer-6

A shorter version of a similar local designer post is dated 20 July 2026 on the same board. Treat them as one hiring effort unless Forest says otherwise.

Hours impact, as an assumption: this is a full-time skilled seat. The expensive minutes are the proposal, the field measure, and the redo when the opening or the appliance changes. How many proposals they write in a month was not published, so this is not a dollar model. It is larger than a missed Saturday phone call at a taproom, because they are spending a hire on it.

**2. Shop drawings go back and forth with the general contractor, and the status is supposed to live in Innergy.**

Forest's LinkedIn page posted a Drafter II hiring note on 17 September 2026 for Weaverville. The Job Network copy of a Drafter II description carries a published date of 14 June 2022. The drafter coordinates standards with the Process Improvement Lead and the Plant Manager. https://www.thejobnetwork.com/job/eb2f966f-637b-45fb-ace8-2ea40500cee3/drafter-ii and https://www.linkedin.com/posts/forest-millwork-the-cabinet-design-studio_hiring-activity-7506431265525518336-JEAJ

Hours impact, as an assumption: each comment round is drafter time plus project-manager time. No wage and no count of comment rounds was published. Innergy is already the shop system. A second system of record would fight Rich Anderson's job.

**3. A stranger can still only start by phone, during shop hours.**

The contact page remains phone and hours. That was the first demo. It is real, and it is the cheap end of the problem. Once a designer is on the phone, the cost is the proposal and the drawing loop above.

No customer-review theme about slow quotes was found. Salary.com-style "employee reviews" were not used. AllBiz addresses from the first memo stayed unused.

### Verdict on the first demo

Sharpen it. A checklist for the estimator is still fair. The recording should show that checklist becoming the start of a pricing proposal a designer would finish, and a status note a drafter could put in Innergy. Stopping at "here are five questions" undersells the hire they just posted and ignores the software they already named.

### Best demo, and what could follow

About two minutes. A sample builder email (a kitchen, fictional) becomes a one-page proposal skeleton: species and finish still blank, appliances listed as questions, field measure flagged, price left blank, and a line that says drafting status stays in Innergy. The last frame names the handoff: designer, then estimator, then drafter. Real: the two shops, Al as owner, the fact that estimating and process improvement are named jobs, and the 2026 designer posting that includes pricing proposals. Sample: the kitchen and the skeleton. No dollar figure.

A larger engagement would connect that proposal skeleton to the Innergy handoff so a drafter is not retyping the same scope, and so a GC comment has a status a project manager can see. Rich Anderson already owns standard procedures. The work would have to make his loop shorter, or he will not want it.

### Risks, overlap, red flags

Rich Anderson is an employee process lead, not a second consultancy. Andy Self's River Arts wood sales are described on the about page as a pastime. Theron Ball's prior shop closed because of Hurricane Helene; that is his old business, not a Forest closure.

No AI title was found. No acquisition was found. The practical risks are size (possibly under the band), a phone-only front door, and a process owner who will reject anything that pretends Innergy does not exist. Confirm the drafter req is still open before mentioning it out loud.
