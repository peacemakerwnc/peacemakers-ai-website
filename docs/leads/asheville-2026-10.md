# Asheville-area leads for Peacemakers AI

Research date: 23 October 2026. Geography: Asheville and Buncombe County, plus Black Mountain, Weaverville, Hendersonville, Waynesville, Brevard, and immediate neighbors (Arden, Fletcher, Fairview, Mills River). Audience: James Fullen, Peacemakers AI. Outreach style assumed: a short screen recording of a working demo in the prospect's own branding. Nothing in this file was built. Demo lines are written concepts only. James approves before any demo, page, or video is made. No one was contacted.

## How this was checked

Every company below was checked, from public pages only, for the miss that showed up on CQC Home:

- In-house operations, technology, or "AI" roles (COO, ops manager, IT, process titles).
- Whether a leader also sells operations consulting or AI implementation.
- Named tools (chat widgets, Calendly, HubSpot, Housecall Pro, JobTread, Buildertrend, ServiceTitan, and similar).
- Red flags: private-equity or strategic acquisition, layoffs, closure, or a business that would put very sensitive records in scope.

Healthcare, dental, behavioral health, pharmacy, legal casework, financial advice, insurance claims, childcare, and HR/payroll services were left out. Property-management ideas below stay on guest messaging and scheduling, not owner bank files, tax IDs, or government IDs.

Revenue is unknown unless a source is named. LinkedIn, Adapt.io, and Datanyze revenue figures are third-party estimates. They often disagree, and they are not company financials. Employee counts on those sites are the same kind of estimate unless the company itself states a number.

Firecrawl was allowed for this task. The Firecrawl CLI was not authenticated in this environment (no API key), so it was not used. Sources are web search results and fetches of public pages.
