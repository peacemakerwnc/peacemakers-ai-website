# North Carolina prospects for Peacemakers AI

Research date: 17 October 2026. This is a prospect screen, not a scored pipeline. The full rows, sources, and caveats are in `nc-prospects-2026-10.csv` beside this note.

Peacemakers AI, run by James Fullen, sells a paid discovery Blueprint at $3,500 and then a monthly "AI department" retainer of about $5,000 to $15,000. The work is the admin stack around building: estimating, proposals, selections, scheduling, job costing, permitting and HOA forms, and reporting. Charter Building Group, a custom home builder at RiverLights in Wilmington, is the reference client and is not on this list.

The target is a company with about $10–50 million in revenue, or about 40–250 employees, that still runs that paperwork with people. National production builders and the giant regional GCs are left off on purpose.

## How to read the sizes

Almost none of these companies publish revenue. Where a dollar figure appears, it is a third-party model, usually ZoomInfo's October 2026 public city lists. Those models repeat the same number across unrelated firms. Several Wilmington companies are all shown at $31.8 million. That is a modeling bucket, not four identical filings.

Employee bands come from RevenueBase public directories (snapshots in September 2026) or from a LinkedIn company page. A band of 11–50 can sit under the 40-person floor. Confidence labels in the CSV mean:

- **medium** — a company statement, a trade profile, or a public LinkedIn headcount, sometimes backed by a second directory
- **low** — one vendor model or a wide employee band
- **unknown** — no figure found

Do not quote a low-confidence dollar figure in an email.

Names are included only when a company page or a named case study lists them. Six companies have a public decision maker. The other 54 do not, in this pass.

One public ERP hit: Saussy Burbank in Charlotte runs Oracle NetSuite with FullClarity's construction tools, live 1 October 2024, according to a FullClarity case study that quotes CFO Kelly Lew.

## The four regions

Fifteen companies each. Order inside a region follows fit and how much size evidence exists, not a formal score.

### Wilmington / Cape Fear (New Hanover, Brunswick, Pender)

Hagood Homes and Smithson are the two to call first. Monteith Construction, T.A. Woods, Pine Valley Construction, and Civil Works Contracting are the commercial and trade firms with some size evidence. Cape Fear Energy Systems, Highland Roofing, O'Brien Service Company, and Longley Supply cover HVAC, roofing, and distribution. Herrington Classic Homes and PBC Design + Build are the other home-builder fits; their published headcount may be under 40. Zimmer Development (6725 Monument Drive) is the local developer. Catlin Engineers and Scientists is the engineering name. Saffo Contractors is sized ($22.6M model, band 51–200) but it is an industrial coatings firm, not a home builder.

### Charlotte metro

Saussy Burbank is the NetSuite conversation. Copper Builders, Classica Homes, Simonini Homes, and Grandfather Homes (1111 Central Ave #320) are the custom and luxury builders. Edison Foard (model $35.4M) and McFarland (model $38.2M, band 51–200) are the GCs closest to the revenue band. Myers & Chapman is a GC with a 51–200 band and no public revenue. A.M. King (model $65.6M) and Climate Systems (model $56.0M) sit just above the revenue ideal and inside the headcount band. Dilling, Davco Roofing, Henderson Properties, Optima Engineering, and Yandle-Witherspoon Supply cover trades, property management, MEP, and supply. Optima's site lists Charlotte, Raleigh, and a new Asheville office; it is counted once, under Charlotte.

### Triangle (Raleigh, Durham, Cary, Chapel Hill)

Homes By Dickerson, Paragon Custom Homes, and McKee Homes are the top three. Homestead Building Company, Buildsense (Durham), and Robuck Homes are more custom builders with thinner size data. Bobbitt (Cary), C.T. Wilson (Durham), and Cooper Tacia are the commercial contractors in a 51–200 band, without public revenue. AirMakers (model $47.1M), Air Experts, Roofwerks (model $25.7M), and L.L. Vann Electric (model $24.4M) are the trades. TGS Engineers (model $26.9M, band 51–200) is the engineering firm. Rhyne Management is the property manager.

### Western NC / Asheville (Buncombe and nearby)

Public revenue is scarce here. Living Stone, Buchanan Construction, and McGill Associates are the three with a real headcount source. Beverly-Grant is the commercial GC; its own site says "over thirty" people, while a directory says 51–200, so the number is unsettled. Steel Root Builders is the only other custom builder with a 51–200 directory band, and that band needs a check because it looks high. Patton Construction Group, Dunlap Construction in Hendersonville, Red Tree Builders, JAG Construction, Harrison Homes, Copperwood Builders, and Ewing Construction are real local firms with unknown or only a small-band size. Bolton Services of WNC is the HVAC and plumbing name. Carlton Edwards and Ryse Construction are directory-listed Asheville contractors in the 11–50 band; confirm what they build before writing them.

Copperwood Builders in Asheville is not Copper Builders in Charlotte.

## Associations and rooms for a warm intro

These are the local rooms, not a national mailing list. Sites were checked on 17 October 2026.

**Wilmington / Cape Fear**

- Wilmington-Cape Fear Home Builders Association, [wcfhba.org](https://www.wcfhba.org/). A 2024 WilmingtonBiz note described it as the second-largest HBA in the state, with 1,200+ members. Best room for builders, trades, and suppliers.
- Wilmington Parade of Homes, [wilmingtonparadeofhomes.com](https://wilmingtonparadeofhomes.com/). Run with the HBA since 1986. Hagood has won Parade awards. This is the annual walk-through, not a cold list.
- Brunswick County Home Builders Association, [brunswickcountyhba.org](https://www.brunswickcountyhba.org/), Shallotte. Use this for Leland, Southport, and the beaches, which the Wilmington HBA does not fully cover.
- Wilmington Chamber of Commerce, [wilmingtonchamber.org](https://www.wilmingtonchamber.org/).

**Western NC**

- Builders Association of the Blue Ridge Mountains, [bablueridge.com](https://bablueridge.com/) (`ashevillehba.com` redirects here). Freestone's 2026 builder roundup points members here.
- Western North Carolina Parade of Homes, [wncparadeofhomes.com](https://wncparadeofhomes.com/). The 2026 tour is the public builder lineup.
- Green Built Alliance, [greenbuilt.org](https://www.greenbuilt.org/). The directory is where JAG-style high-performance builders and their subs show up.

**Statewide, useful in every region**

## What this pass did not prove

No one on this list was called. No revenue figure above "low" confidence should be treated as a fact. Western North Carolina in particular has famous builders and almost no public revenue. The CSV says "unknown" there instead of guessing.

A sensible first week is the top 10, starting with Hagood, Smithson, and T.A. Woods in Wilmington, then Saussy Burbank because the NetSuite story is already public, then Dickerson, Paragon, and Living Stone.
