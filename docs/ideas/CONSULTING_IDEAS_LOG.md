### Claim check

Influencer claims stay labeled. None of the reach or lead claims were measured.

1. **Search indexing — real control, oversold, often already on.** The toggle exists for public professional accounts (18+). Trade reporting from July 2025 (BestMediaInfo, The Keyword, Kapwing, Distl, Website Builder Expert), citing Instagram’s own notice, says search engines may index public photos, videos, carousels, and Reels from those accounts, and that the toggle is **on by default** with an opt-out. Stories, Highlights, private accounts, and personal accounts stay out. This pass did not retrieve Meta’s help page (about.instagram.com returned 403), so “default on since 10 July 2025” is **INFERRED** from those secondary reports, not verified against Meta’s primary page. “Absolute game changer for reach” is a **HYPOTHESIS**. Indexing makes public captions eligible for Google; it does not rank them. De-indexing after an opt-out is slow and not guaranteed by Meta.

2. **Similar-account suggestions — real desktop checkbox, weak lever.** The Verge (2020) and later how-tos describe a web-only Edit profile option, historically “Similar account suggestions,” that stops your account appearing next to similar profiles. The Reel shows that checkbox unchecked and says to turn it on. Whether a given account already has it on is **UNKNOWN** until that account is opened. Effect size is **UNKNOWN**. It changes suggestion placement, not feed distribution, and it places the account beside whoever Instagram treats as similar.

3. **Minimum age 25 in India, Pakistan, and Brazil — real control, wrong job, unsupported claim.** Professional accounts have had a minimum-age setting since about 2020 so a business can keep an account away from under-age viewers (age-restricted products, local rules). Meta’s stated use is age-appropriateness. “Leads with more money” is a **HYPOTHESIS** with no evidence in the Reel. Using country-age gates as a wealth filter is a brand and ethics problem: it hides the account from everyone under 25 in those countries, including younger owners, staff, and apprentices. Leave this off.

4. **Cross-posting — real, “more views” unproven.** Connecting a Facebook Page and sharing feed posts and Reels is a normal Meta feature. Extra views are a **HYPOTHESIS**. The same file goes to Facebook with no separate edit. People who follow both see a duplicate. Connecting the Page is a publishing-permission change.

5. **Highest upload quality — real, small.** The toggle exists and the Reel ends with it on. Instagram still recompresses. Reach impact is **UNKNOWN** and likely negligible. Cost is extra upload data. Low risk if the account is already posting video.

**Leave alone:** the age gate; treating similar-accounts or cross-posting as a prerequisite to posting; the “never post until” framing.

**Effort:** about 15 minutes on an account he already controls. No build.

**Risk:** age-gate optics; indexing content that was not written to be found on Google; suggestion adjacency; cross-posting duplicates; connecting Facebook without a separate publish decision.
