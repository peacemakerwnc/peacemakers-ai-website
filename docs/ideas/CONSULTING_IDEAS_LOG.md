### Where it fits Peacemakers AI

**Own marketing, and only as a test of distribution.** If a consulting Reel is already earning real saves and profile taps, a small Engagement campaign on that existing post can buy more people seeing it and a thicker like/comment count. That is social proof, not a lead system. It does not sell the $3,500 Blueprint or the $10k / $15k / $20k monthly tiers. Those need a lead, message, or call objective pointed at a real next step.

**Assessment demo, as a warning.** When a builder or home-services owner says they “boost posts,” this is the pattern: Engagement, existing post, no button, broad or interest targeting, vanity results. The assessment question is what the spend produced besides likes. The client system to recommend, if ads are even in scope, is usually the one tied to calls or form fills, after the current CRM and follow-up are clear. Do not sell this Reel’s setup as the client build.

**Not outreach.** It does not produce a list of owners. **Not the internal stack.** Ads Manager is a manual ad account, not an agent or automation. **Not the offer.**

If it were ever tested, the music interests and “high-traffic countries” come out. Geography would be the real service area (North Carolina, or the metros he actually serves). Interests, if the account still allows them, would be owners and operators in trades he serves, or no detailed targeting at all. The promoted post would have to be one that already explains the work in plain language. A “no button” call to action fights lead capture; keep it only if the goal is truly proof on the post, not a booked assessment.

### Effort, risk, and approval

**Effort.** No engineering. A careful Ads Manager setup is a short working session, plus a live check that the July 2026 labels still exist. Creative has to exist first. Someone has to read comments and turn it off. Meta’s learning guidance is about 7 days, so a real read is a week of spend, not an afternoon.

**Money.** $5 is the average he typed, not a ceiling. Plan on up to about $35 in a full week at that setting, and an in-account alert if $5 is under the current minimum. Highest volume with a tiny budget can spend the whole amount quickly on cheap engagement.

**Risk.**

- Optimizes for engagement. A viral-looking Reel can still produce zero Blueprint calls.
- Stale UI. Comments say pieces of this path are already gone.
- Wrong audience. His music interests and foreign “traffic” countries would waste money and make the brand look bought.
- Brand adjacency. He turns multi-advertiser ads off. Leave them off so the consultancy is not sitting in a tile with unrelated advertisers.
- Housing and other special ad categories. The recording shows the declaration box. Ordinary B2B consulting about AI for builders is not a housing, credit, employment, or political ad. An ad that is actually about buying or renting homes is a different case and restricts age, gender, and ZIP targeting. Do not declare housing “just in case,” and do not ignore it if the creative is about housing.
- AI creative disclosure. The ad step shows “Ad includes media created or edited with AI.” If the Reel used generated visuals or voice, that box has to be checked. Do not hide it.
- No client use without a signed engagement, and no promise that a client’s posts will go viral.
