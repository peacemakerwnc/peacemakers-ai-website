### Summary

Rogan reads Holmes's "delete everything, then keep a local copy" posts, agrees, and the conversation runs out into AGI, implants, and a claim that late adopters will be shut out.

### Best takeaway

Keep the line he actually agrees with: know where the data lives, and keep a copy the company controls. Drop the rest. "Public within a year," "AGI is here," "integrate or you won't survive," and a neural implant are the podcast talking. They are not a finding about a contractor, a builder, or a home-services firm.

### How it applies to James's offers

**Blueprint ($3,500).** This is a question inside the review Peacemakers already sells, not a new product. `peacemakers-ai/blueprint.html` already includes a practical review of data sensitivity, system access, and third-party tools. Ask where estimates, job photos, customer messages, and files live, who can export them, and whether any of that is being sent to a model the client does not control. "Train your own models" and "buy local compute" are the post's recommendation. The operating standard is current tools first. Local storage is a control question when the data is sensitive. It is not the default build.

**Path in.** No change. Free scorecard, short intro, then the Blueprint when they are ready. Implementation stays a separate decision after the Blueprint (`peacemakers-ai/llms.txt`, `peacemakers-ai/index.html`).

**Own marketing.** Do not repost this Reel, the Holmes screenshots, or the "delete your search history" hook. The footage is someone else's show. The close is fear and profanity. `peacemakers-ai/security.html` already says the practice is client-controlled data, least access, and approval before a change. That page is the public version of the useful line. A future post, only if James writes it, can say a firm should know where its files live before it adds a model. It cannot say non-adopters will not survive, and it cannot promise income, leads, or a gap between haves and have-nots.

**Outreach.** Not a script. Do not open a note to a prospect with Holmes, AGI, or "you will be left out."

**Client system.** Nothing to install and nothing to build from this clip.
