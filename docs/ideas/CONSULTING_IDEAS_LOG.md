**Risk.**

- The income sentences are the ones the FTC has been treating as deceptive when they are sold to small businesses. The practice playbook (PR #30, research date 17 October 2026) cites the FTC's "Keep your AI claims in check" (27 February 2023) and Operation AI Comply, including the Air AI matter. "This will pay for itself" and "top 1%" do not go in a Peacemakers deck. A podcast's hour-count does not either.
