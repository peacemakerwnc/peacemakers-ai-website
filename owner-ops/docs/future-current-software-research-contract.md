# Future current-software research contract

**Status:** Documented only. Do not research or recommend tools in Phase 1.1.

## When research is required

Time-sensitive software recommendations (capabilities, pricing, plan/tier, integrations, APIs, limits, security posture, discontinued features) must be based on research at generation or refresh time — not training-cut knowledge alone.

## Each time-sensitive finding must preserve

- Research date
- Source URL
- Source title
- Relevant product
- Relevant plan/tier
- Verified or uncertain status
- Researcher or process
- Client-specific validation requirement
- Expiration / reverification expectation where appropriate

## Research process (future)

1. Analyze the current stack.
2. Determine whether existing systems can meet the need.
3. Research the current stack first.
4. Create the smallest credible alternative shortlist.
5. Research only that shortlist.
6. Compare cost, value, complexity, supportability, and implementation fit.
7. Require James’s approval.

Do not search the entire software market for every problem.
