05

Scope of work

Phase 1 — included when that line is authorized

  1. Review the 7 October tile samples and lock a tile field schema. Lock one ARC / exterior form schema when Charter provides the form version to use.
  2. Build a Charter-branded fillable / printable tile worksheet and a PDF / palette path, including rooms with more than one option.
  3. Build the ARC / exterior worksheet for that one form, plus a consistent visual packet.
  4. Up to two rounds of brand look-and-feel revision.
  5. Pilot on one or two jobs Charter names. Anne confirms the Buildertrend upload path.
  6. One training walkthrough for Anne and the designers, plus a short written how-to, a human-review checklist, and support through acceptance.

Phase 1b — included only when that line is authorized

  1. Paint worksheet on the accepted Phase 1 pattern, using the completed HLRL9017 paint sheet and a blank template or a written column confirmation from Anne.
  2. Electrical assist that connects the master list to the job sheet and produces the client view and the electrician view from the two templates Anne sent.
  3. Incremental how-to notes and acceptance support for those two outputs.

Buildertrend and Sage 100 Contractor stay in place. Phase 1 does not write selections back into Buildertrend through an API.

Schedule and start conditions

After kickoff, tile and ARC are typically about 4 to 6 weeks. Paint and electrical, once the Phase 1 pattern is accepted, are typically about 2 to 3 weeks. If both lines start together, a typical elapsed window is about 6 to 9 weeks. These are ranges. They move when reviews, pilot jobs, or missing source files move.

Work begins only after all of the following:

  1. Both parties sign this Agreement.
  2. Peacemakers receives the required initial payment in cleared funds under the option checked in Section 11.
  3. Charter supplies the kickoff items in Sections 8 and 10 that the first worksheet needs. Tile can start from the files already received. ARC, paint, and photos start when those files are in hand.

The signature record is the signed copy of this Agreement. Payment is confirmed by the invoice record, not by this document alone.

Acceptance

The authorized work is complete when all of the following are true:

  • Each authorized worksheet matches the frozen schema and the agreed look and feel, within the two included revision rounds.
  • Pilot PDFs or palettes for one or two jobs are prepared.
  • Anne confirms the Buildertrend upload path works for those pilot files.
  • If Phase 1b is authorized: the paint template matches the Phase 1 pattern, and the electrical dual views for the pilot job are produced from the master list without hand-editing columns. People still check prices.
  • The training session and handoff notes have been delivered.
  • Holly (business) and Anne (process) sign the acceptance record.

Charter will accept in writing, or send specific deficiencies, within 10 business days after Peacemakers gives notice that the deliverables are ready. If that window passes with no written objection, or if Charter uses the deliverables in production without a written objection, the notified items may be treated as accepted.

Change control
