## 5. Scope of work

**Phase 1 — included when that line is authorized**

1. Review the 7 October tile samples and lock a tile field schema. Lock one ARC / exterior form schema when Charter provides the form version to use.
2. Build a Charter-branded fillable / printable tile worksheet and a PDF / palette path, including rooms with more than one option.

4. Up to two rounds of brand look-and-feel revision.
5. Pilot on one or two jobs Charter names. Anne confirms the Buildertrend upload path.
6. One training walkthrough for Anne and the designers, plus a short written how-to, a human-review checklist, and support through acceptance.

**Phase 1b — included only when that line is authorized**

1. Paint worksheet on the accepted Phase 1 pattern, using the completed HLRL9017 paint sheet and a blank template or a written column confirmation from Anne.
2. Electrical assist that connects the master list to the job sheet and produces the client view and the electrician view from the two templates Anne sent.
3. Incremental how-to notes and acceptance support for those two outputs.

Buildertrend and Sage 100 Contractor stay in place. Phase 1 does not write selections back into Buildertrend through an API.
