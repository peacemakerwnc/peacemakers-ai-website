## The Sage rule

**Nothing in Phase 1 may block a later purchase-order and Sage integration.**

Sage 100 Contractor stays the accounting and job-cost system. Buildertrend stays the system for jobs, the live schedule, selections, client approval, and selection or subcontractor purchase orders. Angie keys approved changes into Sage for job cost. Phase 1 produces packets a person reviews and Anne uploads. That handoff stays intact.

Anne’s point on the call, and Angie’s note the same day, still govern the build: worksheets are the first win. Purchase orders and invoices are the further step. A matching shortcut, a revived Smartsheet purchase-order file, or a half-finished SharePoint pilot would have to be thrown away when Sage is connected. Leave those alone.

A Phase 1 file fails this checklist if it does any of these:

- Creates, matches, or posts a purchase order or an invoice.
- Writes into Sage, or writes selections into Buildertrend through an API.
- Restarts Smartsheet, or the SharePoint purchase-order pilot that never finished.
- Treats a worksheet price as an accounting entry.
- Becomes the only copy of a fact Buildertrend or Sage already owns.

Prices on a packet are for a person to review. A field that exists only to feed Sage waits for Angie’s screen-share. Angie reported the Sage company name as Donaldson Construction & Management. Phase 1 proceeds without an import file. Design one only after she confirms that company name on the screen-share.

Every item below has the same extra done-test: the output still passes this rule.

**Inputs needed from Charter**

- `Tile SS Template.xlsx` and `HLRL9017 Tile Selection Sheet.xlsx` (received).

- One or two pilot job names, and the designer who will fill them.

A Charter-branded tile worksheet and a client PDF / palette. Rooms with more than one option stay as options. Anne uploads the reviewed file. Buildertrend remains the approval record.

**Owner**

James builds it. Anne confirms the field list and the upload.

**Done-test**

- [ ] The written field list matches the template and the HLRL9017 sheet, and Anne has agreed to it.
- [ ] A pilot packet shows every location on that sheet, including a location with more than one option.
- [ ] The PDF uses Charter’s branding and images Charter has the right to use.

- [ ] Anne has uploaded it on a pilot job, or has walked the upload and confirmed the path.
- [ ] The Sage rule at the top of this list still passes.

**Open risks**

- Freezing columns from the call memory. The schema comes from the two files Anne sent.
- A palette that changes shape from job to job because it was drafted in a general chat with no field list. Assistive drafting is allowed inside the worksheet. A person still reviews the packet.
- Missing photos. The visual packet cannot ship on placeholders Charter does not own.
- Treating the PDF as the client’s signature. Formal approval stays in Buildertrend.
