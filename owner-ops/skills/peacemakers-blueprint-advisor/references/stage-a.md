# Stage A — Questionnaire Diagnostic

## Purpose

Turn a submitted questionnaire into a disciplined discovery plan. Do not finalize root causes, solution architecture, vendors, AI use, implementation effort, savings, timeline, or price. Aligns with Stage A in [CONSULTING_STANDARD.md](../../docs/peacemakers-operating-system/CONSULTING_STANDARD.md).

## Minimum inputs

- Submitted questionnaire or a faithful extract.
- Client and engagement name if available.
- Any artifacts supplied with the questionnaire.

If the questionnaire itself is missing, stop and request it. Do not reconstruct it from memory.

## Output contract

Begin with `Stage A — Questionnaire Diagnostic` and produce:

1. **Decision gate** — choose `Ready for focused Blueprint call`, `Request minimum evidence first`, or `Insufficient intake`; explain why.
2. **Evidence register** — inventory supplied items and classify each with the canonical OS classes: VERIFIED, CLIENT_REPORTED, OBSERVED, INFERRED, HYPOTHESIS, ASSUMPTION, CONTRADICTED, UNKNOWN.
3. **Outcome and scope** — restate desired business outcomes, stakeholders, affected processes, constraints, and exclusions. Mark any client wording that remains vague.
4. **Initial process inventory** — summarize each named process with trigger, owner, major steps, systems, output, customer, reported pain, and evidence class. Do not fill gaps with typical-industry assumptions.
5. **Diagnostic hypotheses** — list a small number of plausible explanations as HYPOTHESIS items, each with supporting evidence, contrary evidence, and a discriminating question or artifact. Do not label hypotheses as findings.
6. **Unknowns and conflicts** — rank only the gaps (UNKNOWN) and conflicts (CONTRADICTED) that could change the call, recommendation, risk, or estimate.
7. **Minimum evidence request** — request the smallest safe artifact or demonstration for each material gap. State why it matters and offer a lower-burden alternative. Prefer redacted samples and screenshots. Do not request credentials.
8. **Focused 90-minute Blueprint-call agenda** — allocate exactly 90 minutes. Center the agenda on the two or three highest-value uncertain workflows, include the questions or demonstrations needed to test hypotheses, identify the expected decision from each segment, and reserve time for measures, constraints, and next steps.
9. **Call preparation** — state what James should review, what the client should have available, and which stakeholders should attend because they perform or own the priority work.
10. **Next action** — name the one immediate action and James's decision.

## Blueprint Call expectations (prep only)

The agenda and evidence requests should prepare James to validate on the call:

- process walkthrough (documented vs actual)
- exceptions, controls, handoffs, delays, rework
- source of truth / system of record
- ownership and decision rights
- measurable baselines where possible
- contradictions and how they resolve
- “how do you know it’s working?”

Do not treat the call itself as Stage B output.

## Guardrails

- Findings remain provisional until validated in later stages.
- Treat the current software's plan, configuration, adoption, training, reporting, automation, and integration capabilities as UNKNOWN until evidenced.
- Do not recommend a new CRM, AI tool, automation platform, or core-system replacement.
- Do not provide unsupported AI recommendations.
- Do not provide vendor rankings or comparisons.
- Do not fabricate volumes, cycle times, error rates, staffing, savings, revenue, ROI, implementation effort, duration, target KPIs, fixed timeline, quote, or price.
- Allow a no-regret process clarification only when it is reversible, costs nothing material, and does not prejudge the Blueprint.
- Value at this stage is CLIENT_REPORTED / UNKNOWN estimates only — never a sales claim.
